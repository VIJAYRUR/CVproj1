Annotated Bibliography

1
Saputra, M. E., Susanto, A., and Carmelita, B. J. 2024. Implementation of Tesseract OCR and Bounding Box for Text Extraction on Food Nutrition Labels. Building of Informatics, Technology and Science (BITS) 6, 3 (Dec. 2024), 1403–1412. DOI: https://doi.org/10.47065/bits.v6i3.6107
This paper presents an OCR pipeline using Tesseract with bounding box detection for reading food nutrition labels. It discusses preprocessing techniques and evaluates recognition accuracy on packaging data.
Reliability: Peer-reviewed journal article, recent (2024), strong technical contribution.
2
Grumeza, T.-R., Bozga, B., and Stăniloiu, G.-L. 2024. Integration of LLM in Expiration Date Scanning for Visually Impaired People. Scalable Computing: Practice and Experience 25, 6 (Oct. 2024), 5722–5733. DOI: https://doi.org/10.12694/scpe.v25i6.4967
Introduces a system combining EasyOCR and large language models (LLMs) for robust expiration date detection on product labels. The study focuses on improving accessibility for blind users.
Reliability: Peer-reviewed journal, 2024, applied directly to accessibility.
3
Olumide, M. 2024. Leveraging OCR for Real-Time Information Retrieval in Complex Environments. ResearchGate Preprint (Dec. 2024). Available at: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/387060469
Surveys deep learning methods (CNNs, RNNs, YOLO) for real-time OCR under challenging conditions such as motion blur and poor lighting. Discusses edge computing and deployment aspects.
Reliability: Preprint (not peer-reviewed), but technically detailed and recent (2024). Useful for state-of-the-art context.
4
Bhagat, S., Joshi, P., Agarwal, A., and Gupta, S. 2023. Accessibility Evaluation of Major Assistive Mobile Applications Available for the Visually Impaired. ITU Journal on Future and Evolving Technologies 4, 4 (Dec. 2023), 628–640. DOI: https://doi.org/10.23919/ITUJ-FET.2023.1001234
Evaluates popular assistive apps like Seeing AI, Lookout, and Envision AI. Compares them on accuracy, speed, usability, and accessibility features with feedback from visually impaired users.
Reliability: Peer-reviewed ITU journal, strong applied evaluation.
5
Johan, E. B. and Rizal, A. 2021. Allergen Recognition in Food Ingredients with Computer Vision. Ultima Computing: Jurnal Sistem Komputer 13, 2 (Dec. 2021), 44–49. DOI: https://doi.org/10.31937/sk.v13i2.2051
Proposes a system using OCR and string matching to identify allergens in food ingredient lists. Demonstrates use cases for mobile assistive applications.
Reliability: Peer-reviewed regional journal, slightly older (2021) but relevant to allergen accessibility.
