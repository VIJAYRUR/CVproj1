Introduction

Empowering Independence Through Computer Vision

Discover how modern AI and computer vision can transform food label accessibility for blind and visually impaired individuals.

Background and Motivation

Food labels provide critical details such as ingredients, nutritional facts, allergens, and expiration dates. For people who are blind or visually impaired, this information is often inaccessible because of small font sizes, cluttered layouts, curved surfaces, and poor lighting conditions.

Missing this information can directly impact dietary choices and health outcomes. The emergence of computer vision and artificial intelligence (AI) has created new opportunities to make food label information accessible through innovative technological solutions.

The Core Problem

Accessible food label reading has three main challenges:

Extracting Text

Reading text on packages with OCR. Handling poor print quality, noise, and odd layouts.

Understanding Content

Finding what really matters (allergens, expiry date) instead of raw text.

Delivering Accessibly

Using voice assistants and multimodal feedback so blind users can interact naturally.

What Research Tells Us

Allergen Detection

2023

App Evaluations

Bhagat et al. studied apps like Seeing AI and Lookout. Found they are helpful but often slow or inaccurate [4].

2024

Smarter OCR on Labels

Saputra et al. used Tesseract with bounding boxes for nutrition labels [1].
Grumeza et al. combined EasyOCR with LLMs for expiry date recognition [2].
Olumide surveyed deep learning OCR (CNNs, YOLO, RNNs) for real-world environments [3].
